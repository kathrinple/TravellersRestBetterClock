Better Clock - Changelog
========================

v1.3.2
------
- Fixed: The speed up, slow down, and pause hotkeys were not working.
  You can now press F9 to cycle through normal / fast / slow time speed,
  and assign a pause key in the config file to pause/unpause time.

v1.3.1
------
- Fixed: Clock was showing 00:00 when other mods (e.g. FishingTweaks)
  interfered with Harmony patches.
- Fixed: The "you are tired" warning emoji now appears correctly again.
- Reduced log spam.

v1.3.0
------
- Updated fork of Better Clock by DrStalker.
- Fixed compatibility with FishingTweaks mod.
- Eliminated clock flickering.
- Added option to show three-character weekday names (e.g. "Wed" instead of "We").
